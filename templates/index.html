How to Export Raw Email Source
  1. Open the email in Gmail on your web browser.
  2. Click the three dots (More) in the top-right corner of the email message.
  3. Select "Show original".
  4. Click "Download Original" to get the .eml file, or click "Copy to clipboard" to paste it here.
  1. Open the email in Outlook (web or desktop).
  2. Click the three dots (...) in the message action bar.
  3. Go to View → View message source.
  4. Select all text (Ctrl+A), copy it, and paste it into MailTrust.
  1. Select the email in Apple Mail.
  2. Choose View → Message → Raw Source from the top menu bar.
  3. Copy the source text or drag the email directly to your desktop to save as an .eml file.
