CNS Mathematical Guarantee:

Asymmetric signatures guarantee non-repudiation: only the holder of the private key can produce a valid signature, while any recipient with the published DNS public key can verify it. Altering even a single whitespace or character in transit invalidates the computed SHA-256 body hash.
